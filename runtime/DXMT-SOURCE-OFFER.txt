DXMT — LGPL source offer (Cosmos Runtime)

Cosmos may download and cache DXMT (Direct3D→Metal translation for Wine on macOS)
as part of the Cosmos Runtime. DXMT releases after v0.80 are licensed under the
GNU Lesser General Public License (LGPL).

Upstream project: https://github.com/3Shain/dxmt

Corresponding source for the DXMT version pinned in runtime/cosmos-runtime.json
is available from the upstream repository and release tags at:

  https://github.com/3Shain/dxmt/releases

To obtain source for a specific DXMT version installed on your Mac, match the
version shown by:

  ./run.command --status

with the release tag at the URL above (for example, v0.81).

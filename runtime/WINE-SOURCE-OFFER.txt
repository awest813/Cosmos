Wine — LGPL source offer (Cosmos Runtime)

Cosmos downloads macOS Wine builds (Gcenx tarballs) as part of the Cosmos Runtime.
Wine is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later
(LGPL-2.1+).

Upstream Wine source: https://gitlab.winehq.org/wine/wine

Gcenx macOS packaging (binaries Cosmos pins in runtime/cosmos-runtime.json):
  https://github.com/Gcenx/macOS_Wine_builds/releases

To obtain source matching the Wine version installed on your Mac:

  1. Run: ./run.command --status
  2. Note the Wine version (for example 11.8).
  3. Download the corresponding Wine source from WineHQ for that major release, or
     consult the Gcenx release notes for the exact upstream Wine commit.

WineHQ source archives: https://www.winehq.org/git

LGPL impact (summary): Cosmos (LGPL-3.0+) and Wine (LGPL-2.1+) are both copyleft
runtimes. You may replace the Wine build with your own LGPL-compliant build. If you
redistribute a Cosmos Runtime bundle that includes Wine binaries, include this file,
runtime/NOTICE.md, and LICENSE — see docs/LGPL_IMPACT.md.
